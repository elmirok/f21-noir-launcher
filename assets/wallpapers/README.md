## Production prompt

Create an original, cinematic neo-noir lock-screen wallpaper for a compact 480×640 Android phone.
Use nearly black futuristic architecture, fine rain, atmospheric haze, wet pavement, restrained
amber light and a single subtle deep-red point. Keep the upper 45 percent almost entirely black and
uncluttered for a large white clock, keep the middle subdued for notification cards, and concentrate
detail and reflections in the lower quarter. Do not include people, vehicles, UI, text, numbers,
letters, logos, watermarks, blue/cyan gradients, recognizable copyrighted designs or busy neon signs.
